Unregister {{.Title}}?

aimd forgets this plan. There's no Undo.

  • It leaves the dashboard and the menu.
  • Its packages, links and history go: every link (the ones you made by hand too), your unlinks, Mark done and what you hid from Unmatched, the timeline and its alerts, and who merges if you changed it in Settings.
  • Its lead session is no longer monitored as a lead, nor its agents as the plan's agents. A session you watch stays watched.
  • The plan file stays as it is in the repo, and its branches, worktrees and PRs are untouched.

Registering the file again starts afresh: aimd matches its PRs and branches again, but agent links, your links and unlinks, Mark done and the history don't come back. To register it again, run this in a Claude Code session:

/plan-register {{.PathFull}}
